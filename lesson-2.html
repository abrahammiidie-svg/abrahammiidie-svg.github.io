Having a clear vision of what you want to achieve is crucial for success. Without a vision, every opportunity looks equally attractive, and every distraction feels urgent. With a vision, you can ask one useful question: does this move me closer to my quest, or further from it?

Without a vision, every opportunity looks equally attractive, and every distraction feels urgent. With a vision, you can ask one useful question: does this move me closer to my quest, or further from it?

That is why I believe a man must have a quest. A quest is more than a goal. A goal is a single target, like passing an exam. A quest is a long journey that gives your goals a reason. It is what makes the hard days worth it.

I was instrospecting the other day about why I am always beating myself up and indulging myself in things that I know I ought not to be doing. It occurred to me that I have no quest that I candidly deemed meaningful. One that metaphorically speaking, I'm willing to give my bleed, sweat and give up my rationality for.I had no vision. I had no reason to say no to the things that are not good for me. I have no reason to say yes to the things that are good for me. I had no reason to be disciplined. I had no reason to be courageous. I had no reason to be wise. I had no reason to be temperate. I had no reason to be just. I had no reason to be a better man than I was yesterday. In short, I become a danger to myself when I don't have a quest.

A vision alone is not enough, though. This is where many people go wrong. Dreaming about the future feels good, but nothing changes until the dream becomes small daily actions. The vision shows the destination, and the daily habits are the steps that get you there.

Candidly, we all must have a quest that will keep us focused on the things that matter and help us to say no to the things that don't.
